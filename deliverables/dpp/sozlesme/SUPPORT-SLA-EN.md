# QRNot DPP — Support and Service Level Agreement (SLA)

This SLA is **Annex 2** to the Subscription and Services Agreement (the “Main Agreement”) between SCE INNOVATION LTD. ŞTİ. (“QRNot”) and the Customer. Defined terms have the meaning given in the Main Agreement.

---

## 1. Subject

This SLA sets out:
- support channels and hours;
- response targets;
- the Platform availability target;
- maintenance, backup and restore;
- service credits.

## 2. Channels and hours

1. **Channels:**
   - Email: sce@scegrup.com
   - WhatsApp: +90 543 392 92 30
   - Phone: +90 850 888 1 889 (Growth and above)
   - Enterprise: also a named support contact
2. **Hours:**
   - Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 Turkey time (TRT, UTC+3), excluding Turkish public holidays.
   - Enterprise P1: every day including weekends, 08:00–22:00 TRT.
3. Each request is logged when received and given a ticket number.
4. “Business hours” and “business days” refer to these hours.

## 3. Priority levels

| Level | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **P1 — Critical** | Published passports or the company panel are unavailable for all users | Passport pages return errors; nobody can sign in |
| **P2 — High** | A core function does not work and there is no workaround | Publishing, serial generation or the supplier portal fails; restore request |
| **P3 — Normal** | Partial failure, or a workaround exists | An export fails; display issue |
| **P4 — Request** | Usage question or feature request | “How do I…?” |

## 5. Availability target

1. Monthly availability target for public passport pages and the company panel:

| Plan | Target |
|---|---|
| Pilot, Starter, Growth, Business | **99.5%** |
| Enterprise | **99.7%** |

2. Excluded from the calculation:
   - (a) Planned maintenance announced under clause 6.
   - (b) Issues caused by the Customer's network, devices or misuse.
   - (c) Force majeure.
   - (d) Third-party AI functions such as automatic translation.
   - (e) Suspension under the Main Agreement.

## 6. Planned maintenance

1. Planned maintenance is scheduled, where possible, on weekdays between 22:00 and 06:00 TRT.
2. It is announced at least **48 hours** in advance.
3. It is limited to 4 hours per month.
4. Emergency security maintenance may be carried out without notice. That time counts towards the availability calculation.

## 7. Backup and restore

1. All DPP data is backed up automatically every day. Daily backups are kept for **30 days**.
2. Deleted documents and images remain restorable for **30 days**.
3. Every change is recorded in an audit log with user and time. Deletions keep a copy of the deleted record.
4. Every published passport version is stored immutably.
5. Restore requests are handled as **P2**.
   - Recovery point objective (RPO): 24 hours.
   - Recovery time objective (RTO): 1 business day.
   - One restore per month is free. Further restores are charged at €60/hour.
6. The Customer can export machine-readable copies (passport JSON, serial CSV) at any time and is encouraged to keep its own archive.

## 8. Service credits

1. If the monthly availability target is missed, the Customer may request a credit of that month's subscription fee:

| Monthly availability | Credit |
|---|---|
| Below target, ≥ 99.0% | 5% |
| < 99.0%, ≥ 97.0% | 10% |
| < 97.0% | 25% |

2. Credits apply to Growth, Business and Enterprise plans.
3. Credits must be requested in writing within **30 days** after month end. They are applied to the next invoice and are not paid in cash.
4. Credits are capped at 25% of the monthly fee.
5. Credits are the sole remedy for missed availability. Availability below 97% in three consecutive months is a material breach under the Main Agreement.

## 9. Scope

**Included:**
- Usage questions.
- Bug reports and fixes.
- Access and password issues.
- Restores under clause 7.
- Helping the Customer with suppliers' technical questions.
- Up to 2 security questionnaires per year.

**Not included (quoted separately, €60/hour):**
- Entering product data (data entry service: €20 per model).
- Custom development, integrations and custom reports.
- The Customer's own systems (ERP, label printers, network).
- Regulatory or legal advice.
- Third-party software or hardware.
- Training beyond the plan allowance.

## 11. Customer responsibilities

1. Name at least one technical contact.
2. Provide in each request:
   - a description of the issue;
   - the affected product or passport URL;
   - screenshots;
   - the start time of the issue.
3. QRNot may view panel data to investigate an issue only with the Customer's explicit permission, and records such access in the ticket.
