Give your gift a VOICE. 🎙️

Record a voice message, add a video or photos — then print your personal QR code on a gift tag, card or sticker. When they scan it with their phone camera, they hear YOU. No app needed.

WHAT YOU GET
✔ Your own unique QR code, linked to your QRNot account
✔ Voice messages, videos, photos, Spotify, YouTube and other links

HOW IT WORKS
1. Purchase and download the instruction PDF
2. Go to qrnot.com/etsy and enter your Etsy order number
   (your order is ready to activate within 24 hours)
3. Choose "Voice / video message QR" and click Activate
4. Sign in or create your free QRNot account — your QR is linked to it, so only you can edit it
5. Record your message or upload a video and photos
6. Download your QR and print the templates at home (A4 or US Letter)
