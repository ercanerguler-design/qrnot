Lost? Scan me. 🐾

A QR code for your pet's collar, your child's backpack, your luggage or keys. Whoever finds it scans the code with their phone camera and can call you with one tap.

WHAT THE FINDER SEES
✔ "Call owner" button with your phone number
✔ "Open location" button with the address you entered (opens in Maps)
✔ Your pet's name and a short note
✔ Optional photos and a voice note from the family

WHAT YOU GET
✔ Your own unique QR code, linked to your QRNot account
✔ Update your phone number or address any time — the printed QR never changes
✔ Printable round stickers (12 per page) and tags
✔ Works on iPhone and Android, the finder needs no app

HOW IT WORKS
1. Purchase and download the instruction PDF
2. Go to qrnot.com/etsy and enter your Etsy order number
   (your order is ready to activate within 24 hours)
3. Choose "Voice / video message QR" and click Activate
4. Sign in or create your free QRNot account — your QR is linked to it, so only you can edit it
5. Open "Emergency info" and add your phone number and address
6. Download your QR and print the stickers or tags

GOOD TO KNOW
• There is no GPS tracking. The finder sees the contact info and address you entered.
• For outdoor use, print on waterproof sticker paper or laminate the tag.

USE IT FOR
Dog & cat collars · Kids' backpacks · Luggage · Keys · Medical info cards · Bikes & scooters

IMPORTANT
• This is a DIGITAL product. No physical item will be shipped.
• One unique QR code per item purchased. Buying 3? You get 3 QR codes.
• A free QRNot account is required to set up and manage your QR.
• Your QR stays active as long as your QRNot account and QR exist. You can delete your content any time.
