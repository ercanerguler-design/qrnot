## 2. Ground rules

**Links**
- Overview: `qrnot.com/dpp?lang=en`
- Live sample passport: `qrnot.com/p/ornekpasaport?lang=en` (opens on any phone; switch language top right)
- Contact: WhatsApp +90 543 392 92 30 · sce@scegrup.com

**Say it accurately** (applies to every post and reply)
- ✅ "DPP-**ready** infrastructure", "start collecting and publishing your passport data today"
- ✅ Only these firm dates: ESPR entered into force in **July 2024**; **battery passport from February 2027**
- ❌ Never say "EU-compliant / certified / approved". There is no official approval.
- ❌ No firm dates for textiles, furniture etc. Say "set by upcoming delegated acts".
- ❌ No prices. Say "quoted by product count".
- ❌ Do not tag competitors, EU institutions or individuals.

**Tone:** Calm, informative, professional. No fear-selling ("you'll be fined"); focus on opportunity and readiness.

**Hashtags**
- LinkedIn: 3–5
- Instagram: 5–10
- TikTok: 3–5

Core set: `#DigitalProductPassport #DPP #ESPR #Ecodesign #CircularEconomy`
Add by sector: `#Textiles #Fashion #Furniture #Batteries #Sustainability`

### 06 — "No carbon figure yet? Start with a sourced estimate."

**LinkedIn** (`4x5/06.png`)
> "We don't have a carbon figure for this product yet." That's the honest answer for most SMEs, and that's fine.

> In QRNot DPP, a carbon estimate helper calculates a range for the raw-material (fibre) stage from material shares and product weight, with sources for every value. The passport labels it clearly as an estimate.
>
> When you have a product-specific LCA, you replace it with your declared value. Transparency beats false precision.
>
> #DigitalProductPassport #CarbonFootprint #Sustainability #Textiles

## 6. Replying to comments and messages

**"Is this mandatory?"**
> It is being phased in by product group under the EU's ESPR. The date for battery passports is set (February 2027); dates for other groups will be fixed by the relevant acts. Happy to check the current status for your product group — send us a DM.

**"How much does it cost?"**
> We quote by number of product models; serialised QR codes are unlimited. We can start with a small pilot — DM us and we'll share the details.

**"We can build this on our own website."**
> Absolutely possible. With us, versioning, permanence, 8 languages, authorised access, per-serial pages, GS1 Digital Link and label production are ready out of the box. Take a look at the sample: qrnot.com/p/ornekpasaport?lang=en

**"Is the panel available in English?"**
> Yes. The company panel works in 8 languages (English, German, French, Spanish, Dutch, Arabic, Russian and Turkish). Each user picks their own language, and messages appear in it too.

**First DM reply**
> Hello, thanks for your interest! Which product groups do you sell, and to which EU countries? A short call is enough to outline a pilot for you.
